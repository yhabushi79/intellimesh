The Issue

Two platforms independently manage the same infrastructure. Each one reports success — but neither sees the whole picture.

  • Red Hat Satellite enforces a stricter OS crypto policy (FUTURE:CLASSICAL-COMPAT) on both nodes. Reports "Policy applied successfully".
  • AAP applies a "TLS compliance fix" to Node 2 (patched) only: a 6144-bit RSA certificate and disabled SSL session caching. Reports "Job completed successfully".
  • Result: both platforms report green, all health checks pass, error rate is 0%. But every TLS handshake on Node 2 is ~20x slower — customers see intermittent payment slowness with nothing in the logs pointing at the cause.
